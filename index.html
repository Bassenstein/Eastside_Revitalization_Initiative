UnderstandingCDBG

Cleveland receives roughly $19.7 million in federal Community Development Block Grant funds every year. Here's what it is, how the City decides where it goes, and the three things we're pushing it to fund in our neighborhoods — with residents in the room.

A new public-comment period is opening as City Council reconvenes this fall. This is the window to weigh in before the money is locked in.

Ask #1 · A plan any ward could use

Ward Vacant Lot Stabilization Plan

Using Ward 8 only as an example: a $1,095,000 approach to stabilizing 120–150 hazardous vacant parcels — debris removal, soil remediation, forestry, and perimeter safety, all as blight elimination. The same approach could be applied in any ward by substituting that ward's own parcels.
